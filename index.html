Class 1 · Pronunciation

💡 Kinyarwanda is very consistent — once you learn the rules, words are pronounced clearly and predictably.

The 5 Vowels — always the same

VowelSoundExample
Alike "ah" in fatherNa
Elike "eh" in bedNeza
Ilike "ee" in seeNitwa
Olike "oh" in goMurakoze
Ulike "oo" in foodMuri

Key Letter Combinations

💡 These are the sounds that trip people up most. Practice these and the rest falls into place.
LettersSoundExampleHear it as
RWroll R + W togetherKinyarwandar-wanda
NYlike "ny" in canyonYanyuyah-nyoo
CYlike "ch" in cheekCyizachee-zah
SHlike "sh" in shoeNishimiyenee-shee-mee-yeh
NKnasal "nk" — nose + kIkinyarwandaee-kee-nyar-wanda
NGlike "ng" in singMirongomee-rong-go
NZnasal "nz" — nose + zNezaneh-zah
MU"moo" — lips closed then openMurakozemoo-rah-koh-zeh

Syllable Rules

💡 Every syllable in Kinyarwanda is short and clean. Each one gets equal weight — no syllable is swallowed or skipped.
2
Vowels never blend together
Each vowel has its own beat
Nishimiye = Ni · shi · mi · ye (4 syllables)
3
Consonant clusters stay together
RW, NY, CY etc. are one sound
Kinyarwanda = Ki · nya · rwa · nda (4 syllables)

Class 6 · Conversation

💡 Each exchange has 3 lines: question → teacher answers + asks you back → your turn to answer with your own details.

New Words

1

Standalone · e.g. Njyewe nitwa [your name]
2
We / Us (emphatic)
Twebwe
Standalone · e.g. Twebwe duturuka muri Canada
3
Me too / As for me (in responses)
Nanjye
Used when responding · e.g. Nanjye nimeza
4
Us too / As for us (in responses)
Natwe
Used when responding · e.g. Natwe tumeze neza
5

Wowe?
6
And you? (polite / plural)
Mwebwe?

The Conversation

1 · Name (singular)
🧑
Question
Witwa nde?
What's your name?
👤
Answer + ask back
Nitwa [your name]. Wowe witwa nde?
My name is [your name]. And you, what's your name?
🧑
Your turn
Nitwa [your name].
My name is [your name].
2 · Name (polite)
🧑
Question
Amazina yawe ni ayahe?
What is your name?
👤
Answer + ask back
Amazina yanjye ni [your name]. Mwebwe amazina yanyu ni ayahe?
My name is [your name]. And you all, what are your names?
🧑
Your turn

My name is [your name].
