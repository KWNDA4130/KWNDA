Time-Based Greetings & Farewells

💡 Kinyarwanda has specific greetings for each time of day — and separate phrases for greeting vs. leaving.
Time of DayKinyarwandaMeaning
Morning (greeting) Mwaramutse neza? Did you wake up well?
Afternoon (greeting) Mwiriwe neza? Is your day going well?
Evening (greeting) Mwiriwe mute? Was your day going well?
Day goodbye mwirirwe neza. Stay safe the whole day
Night (leaving) Muramuke neza. Stay safe the night until next morning
Bye (leaving) Murabeho neza. Goodbye
Bye (casual) Murabeho. Bye
