🇷🇼 Kinyarwanda

Class 0 · Pronunciation

💡 Kinyarwanda is very consistent — once you learn the rules, every word sounds exactly as written. No silent letters, no exceptions.

The 5 Vowels — always the same

VowelSoundExample
Alike "ah" in fatherNa
Elike "eh" in bedNeza
Ilike "ee" in seeNitwa
Olike "oh" in goMurakoze
Ulike "oo" in foodMuri

Key Letter Combinations

💡 These are the sounds that trip people up most. Practice these and the rest falls into place.
LettersSoundExampleHear it as
RWroll R + W togetherKinyarwandar-wanda
NYlike "ny" in canyonYanyuyah-nyoo
CYlike "ch" in cheekCyizachee-zah
SHlike "sh" in shoeNishimiyenee-shee-mee-yeh
NKnasal "nk" — nose + kIkinyarwandaee-kee-nyar-wanda
NGlike "ng" in singMirongomee-rong-go
NZnasal "nz" — nose + zNezaneh-zah
MU"moo" — lips closed then openMurakozemoo-rah-koh-zeh

Syllable Rules

💡 Every syllable in Kinyarwanda is short and clean. Each one gets equal weight — no syllable is swallowed or skipped.
1
Every letter is pronounced
No silent letters — ever
Murakoze = Mu · ra · ko · ze (4 syllables)
2
Vowels never blend together
Each vowel = its own beat
Nishimiye = Ni · shi · mi · ye (4 syllables)
3
Consonant clusters stay together
RW, NY, CY etc. are one sound
Kinyarwanda = Ki · nya · rwa · nda (4 syllables)
4
Tone matters
High / low pitch can change meaning
Your teacher's voice is the best guide — listen and copy

Class 5 · Conversation

💡 Each exchange has 3 lines: question → teacher answers + asks you back → your turn to answer with your own details.

New Words

1
Me / As for me
Nanjye
2
Us / As for us
Natwe
3
And you? (singular)
Wowe?
4
And you? (polite / plural)
Mwebwe?

The Conversation

1 · Name (singular)
🧑
Question
Witwa nde?
What's your name?
👤
Answer + ask back
Nitwa [name]. Wowe witwa nde?
My name is [name]. And you, what's your name?
🧑
Your turn
Nitwa [your name].
My name is [your name].
2 · Name (polite)
🧑
Question
Amazina yawe ni ayahe?
What is your name?
👤
Answer + ask back
Amazina yanjye ni [name]. Mwebwe amazina yanyu ni ayahe?
My name is [name]. And you all, what are your names?
🧑
Your turn
Amazina yanjye ni [your name].
My name is [your name].
3 · Where are you from? (singular)
🧑
Question
Uturuka he?
Where are you from?
👤
Answer + ask back
Nturuka muri [country]. Wowe uturuka he?
I am from [country]. And you, where are you from?
🧑
Your turn
Nturuka muri [your country].
I am from [your country].
4 · Where are you from? (polite)
🧑
Question
Muturuka he?
Where are you from? (polite)
👤
Answer + ask back
Natwe duturuka muri [country]. Mwebwe muturuka he?
We are from [country]. And you all, where are you from?
🧑
Your turn
Natwe duturuka muri [your country].
We are from [your country].
5 · Where do you live? (singular)
🧑
Question
Utuye he?
Where do you live?
👤
Answer + ask back
Ntuye I [city] muri [country]. Wowe utuye he?

🧑
Your turn
Ntuye I [your city] muri [your country].
I live in [your city], [your country].
6 · Where do you live? (polite)

Class 6 · Greetings Conv.

💡 Waramutse / Wiriwe = singular/informal · Mwaramutse / Mwiriwe = polite/plural. Nawe = and you? (singular) · Namwe = and you? (polite/plural)
1 · Morning greeting (singular)
🧑
Question
Waramutse neza?
Did you wake up well? (singular)
👤
Answer + ask back
Yego naramutse neza. Wowe? or Nawe?
Yes I woke up well. And you?
🧑
Your turn
Yego nanjye naramutse neza.
Yes, as for me I woke up well.
2 · Morning greeting (polite)
🧑
Question
Mwaramutse neza?
Did you all wake up well?
👤
Answer + ask back
Yego twaramutse neza. Namwe? or Mwebwe?
Yes we woke up well. And you all?
🧑
Your turn
Yego natwe twaramutse neza.
Yes, as for us we woke up well.
3 · Afternoon greeting (singular)
🧑
Question
Wiriwe neza?
Is your day going well? (singular)
👤
Answer + ask back

Your turn
Nanjye niriwe neza.
As for me, my day is going well.
